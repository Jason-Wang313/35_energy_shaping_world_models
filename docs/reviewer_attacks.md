# Reviewer Attacks

- This is just passivity control.
- Hamiltonian neural networks already encode energy.
- Prediction RMSE may be enough if the model is accurate.
- The certificate can be wrong under mismatch.
- The evaluation is deterministic rather than a high-fidelity learned benchmark.
- A robust certificate may be safe only because it is too conservative.
- MPC or passivity filters may make the model-side certificate unnecessary.
- The method may not transfer to contact-rich manipulation or hardware.

V3 responses are built into the manuscript:
- The contribution is an interface contract for learned world models.
- Hamiltonian and port-Hamiltonian predictors are included as hostile baselines.
- RMSE-safety decoupling is measured directly.
- Overconfident certificates are included as negative controls.
- Admitted action fraction and return quantify conservatism.
- Hardware and learned-neural claims are explicitly left for future work.
