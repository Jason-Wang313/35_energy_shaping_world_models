# Reviewer Attacks

- This is just a physics prior; where is the new mechanism?
- Passivity and energy shaping are classical control ideas, so why is this a learning paper?
- The evaluation may only show a toy stabilization gain, not real world robot impact.
- If the certificate is approximate, the safety claim may collapse.
- The method may be equivalent to a constrained latent ODE or Hamiltonian network.
- The claimed novelty may be mostly in wording rather than mechanism.
